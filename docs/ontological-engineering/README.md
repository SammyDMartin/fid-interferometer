# Ontological Engineering — Reference Documents

These documents define the ontological engineering framework. They were produced during and after the origin conversation (March 29, 2026) and provide the conceptual foundation for the FID interferometer experiment and the broader research programme.

## Reading Order

1. **01_BRIEFING.md** — What ontological engineering is: treating framework axioms as engineering specifications and looking for exploits. The core intellectual move, success criteria, and historical hit rate.

2. **02_TRL_SCALE.md** — The extended Technology Readiness Level scale, from NASA's standard TRL 1-9 through Drexler's exploratory engineering down to negative TRLs for ontological engineering (TRL 0 to TRL -3). Key insight: ideas at TRL -3 don't feel unlikely, they feel meaningless.

3. **03_PROPOSALS_CATALOG.md** — All eight proposals in the programme: the Eucharist Hack (TRL -2), the Infinite Improbability Drive analysis (TRL 0), Unprobability (TRL -2), the Simulation Stress-Test (TRL -1), the Omni-Experiment (TRL -1), Self-Instantiating Fiction (TRL -2), the Meta-Proposal (TRL -1), and the Incompleteness Exploit (TRL -2).

4. **04_FAILURE_MODES.md** — How ontological engineering attempts fail, with the Gödel attempt as case study. Taxonomy: colourful metaphors disguised as inferences, framework mixing without logical bridges, equivocation on technical terms, vagueness masquerading as generality, reversed burden of proof. Quality criteria for valid proposals.

5. **05_HISTORICAL_PRECEDENTS.md** — Complete TRL -3 to TRL 9 trace for nuclear weapons (from prehistoric stargazing through Democritus, Dalton, Becquerel, Szilard, Fermi, to operational deterrence). Additional precedents: Hobbes→AGI, Bacon→artillery, Lucretius→evolution, Leibniz→computing, Wells→Hiroshima.

6. **06_KEY_FRAMEWORKS.md** — Exploitable frameworks: anthropic reasoning, simulation hypothesis, unitarity/black hole information paradox, Catholic transubstantiation, unprobability, philosophy of fiction, Mathematical Universe Hypothesis, moral realism, many-worlds interpretation.

7. **07_IDEATION_PROTOCOL.md** — Instructions for AI systems attempting to generate proposals. Includes the acid test, the Gödel test, and phase-by-phase approach.

8. **08_ORIGIN_CONVERSATION.md** — Summary of the March 29, 2026 conversation where ontological engineering was named and systematized. Records key developments: the Omni-Experiment, the unitarity wedge, the extended TRL scale, the meta-proposal, the AI acceleration corollary, Claude's failed Gödel attempt, and the campfire phenomenology.
