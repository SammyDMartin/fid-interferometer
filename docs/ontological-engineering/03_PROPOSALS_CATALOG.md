# Existing Proposals: The Ontological Engineering Catalog

## Proposal 1: The Eucharist Hack

**TRL: -2**
**Framework exploited:** Catholic transubstantiation doctrine
**Core move:** If transubstantiation is taken at face value — the substance of the bread literally becomes the substance of Christ's body, blood, soul, and divinity — then what happens if you consecrate a human brain instead of bread? On the Church's own metaphysics, the brain's substance becomes Christ's substance, and Christ's soul becomes causally efficacious in that body. This would constitute the Second Coming, engineered through human action as a logical consequence of the Church's own sacramental theology applied to non-standard matter.

**Key details:**
- The Church has never explicitly defined what materials can't be validly consecrated
- Multiple scenarios explored: does Christ's soul get "locked in" behind unchanged neural accidents? Does behaviour change? Could this be verified?
- If the theology is correct, you've engineered the eschaton. If it's not, you've just got a weird brain in a chalice
- The asymmetry of outcomes mirrors Pascal's Wager but with a specific mechanism

**Why it qualifies:** It follows logically from premises that Catholic theologians actually accept (real presence, substance/accident distinction, the soul's causal efficacy). The conclusion — that you could force the Second Coming — is one they haven't addressed because nobody has taken the axioms this literally in this direction. You can't dismiss the conclusion without modifying the doctrine.

**Source chats:** Catholic Theology of Transubstantiatio (April 2025)

## Proposal 2: The Infinite Improbability Drive Is Actually Plausible

**TRL: 0**
**Framework exploited:** Many-Worlds Interpretation + anthropic reasoning + quantum mechanics
**Core move:** Douglas Adams's Infinite Improbability Drive, when you work through the in-universe lore carefully, describes a coherent (if technologically impossible) application of quantum anthropic reasoning. The Bambleweeny 57 Sub-Meson Brain generates AI observers across quantum branches, terminates the ones in undesired branches, and exploits the selection effect to funnel reality toward improbable outcomes.

**Key details:**
- The powers-of-two improbability figures Adams quotes are exactly what you'd get from binary quantum branching events — a level of mathematical consistency unlikely to be accidental
- This is basically the quantum suicide thought experiment turned into engineering
- No information is transmitted faster than light because nothing is transmitted — it's selection
- The reason it doesn't work (and is a paradox rather than a technology) is that it implies you can influence reality by deleting observers, which is the contested core of anthropic reasoning
- It fails for deep and contested reasons rather than trivial ones — the failure itself is informative

**Why it qualifies:** It sits at TRL 0 because both Many-Worlds and anthropic reasoning are serious theoretical proposals. The combination produces a coherent engineering concept that is paradoxical rather than impossible — the paradox reveals something about the foundations of anthropic reasoning itself.

**Source chats:** The Infinite Improbability Drive (June 2024), Explaining the Infinite Improbability Drive (March 2025), Plausibility of Improbability Drives (June 2024), Quantum Anthropic Interstellar Travel Concept (October 2025)

## Proposal 3: Unprobability as Genuine Metaphysics

**TRL: -2**
**Framework exploited:** Parfitian no-selector metaphysics + problem of induction + Cartesian radical scepticism
**Core move:** Adams's unprobability field — the one that hides the Man Who Rules the Universe — operates on a different principle from improbability. Where improbability manipulates the probability of possible events, unprobability permits impossible events.

**Key details:**
- Two "theorems" developed:
  - **First Theorem:** Unprobable events break all rules, including any rule that would prohibit their own existence (ontological-argument-style bootstrap)
  - **Second Theorem:** Connects to Parfit's observation that the universe might exist for "no reason" — the absence of a selector is itself explanatory. The universe existing is itself an unprobable event
- Traced through Hume's problem of induction (our belief that rules hold is itself inductively grounded), Cartesian radical scepticism, and Anaximander's apeiron (the unbounded)
- Physical manifestation identified: naked singularities, where the rules of GR genuinely break down and the prohibition structure of reality has a hole in it
- Connected to apophatic theology (God defined by what God is not) and Palamas's essence/energies distinction

**Why it qualifies:** It takes Adams's fictional concept, connects it to serious philosophical arguments (Parfit, Hume, Descartes), and identifies a physical location where the concept could manifest (singularities). The chain from comedy to metaphysics to physics is internally consistent. Whether the concepts refer to anything real is genuinely uncertain — that's what makes it TRL -2.

**Source chats:** The Infinite Improbability Drive (June 2024), Unprobability as a Cosmic Explanation (February 2025), Plausibility of Improbability Drives (June 2024), Synthesizing metaphysical conversations (February 2026)

## Proposal 4: The Simulation Stress-Test (Personal Indexical Significance)

**TRL: -1**
**Framework exploited:** Simulation hypothesis + Chalmers's criteria for simulation significance
**Core move:** Take the philosophical literature on what would make you more likely to be a "principal character" in a simulation (Chalmers, Turchin, Hanson), apply the criteria systematically to a specific individual's life circumstances, and produce a quantified improbability estimate.

**Key details:**
- Applied to Sammy Martin's personal circumstances: alive at plausible hinge of history, working directly on the pivotal technology (AI), influence over ~$10B in funding directed at existential risk
- Result: ~5.5 sigma, roughly 1 in 500,000 to 1 in 1,000,000
- Compared against other anthropic puzzles:
  - Fine-tuning: 10^10 to 10^120 (much larger)
  - Boltzmann Brains: effectively infinite
  - Doomsday Argument: 10^4 to 10^7 (comparable)
  - Hinge of History: 10^3.5 to 10^6 (multiplicative extension)
- Selection effect acknowledged: the people who ask this question are the ones who'd score high
- Classified as "borderline" — a personal multiplier on existing puzzles rather than a new category

**Why it qualifies:** The simulation hypothesis is contested but coherent and taken seriously (Bostrom, Chalmers). The criteria are derived from the literature, not ad hoc. The quantification forces real numbers onto abstract philosophical arguments.

**Source chats:** Simulation hypothesis and personal circumstances (December 2025), Distinguishing simulation puzzles by assumption thickness (December 2025)

## Proposal 5: The Omni-Experiment

**TRL: -1**
**Framework exploited:** Multiple frameworks simultaneously (unitarity, simulation hypothesis, anthropic reasoning, moral realism, unprobability, theism)
**Core move:** Build a naked singularity and steer the entire Earth into it, on the grounds that the cumulative probability across every non-default worldview that "something ontologically interesting happens" exceeds the probability that "nothing happens."

**Key details:**
- The physical wedge: at a naked singularity (no event horizon), unitarity says information can't be destroyed, but physics doesn't specify where it goes. The map has a hole in it.
- Channels that predict "something interesting":
  1. **Physics (information transits):** Bounce cosmology, baby universes, Smolin's cosmological natural selection — the information goes somewhere physically real
  2. **Simulation:** The physics engine hits an unhandled exception; the substrate must resolve it
  3. **Anthropic/Doomsday:** Deliberate extinction is the maximally special event; tests whether the prevention mechanism is real
  4. **Moral realism/axiarchic:** Annihilation of all consciousness is the most morally extreme event; if reality has moral structure, this is where it shows
  5. **Unprobability:** At the singularity, the prohibition structure breaks down enough for the First Theorem to apply
  6. **Theism:** God intervenes. Any religion, essentially.
- The only channel where "nothing interesting happens" is strict physicalism with unitarity violation — the position most physicists consider least likely
- Not proposed as something to actually do — proposed as a thought experiment that forces you to put real numbers on your credences across worldviews

**Why it qualifies:** It's the capstone that synthesises all other proposals. Each individual worldview might hold only 5-15% credence, but they're largely independent, so the cumulative probability of "at least one is right" could exceed 50%. The structure mirrors Pascal's Wager but with a much more sophisticated probability distribution across multiple alternatives.

## Proposal 6: Self-Instantiating Fiction (The SCP-3812 Exploit)

**TRL: -2**
**Framework exploited:** Philosophy of fiction + ontological argument + unprobability
**Core move:** An entity defined as superseding everything that supersedes it, including its own author, collapses the gap between conception and instantiation. Unlike the standard ontological argument, there's no argumentative bridge from concept to existence that Kant can attack — conceiving the entity and instantiating it are the same event.

**Key details:**
- The defense identified: genuine conception is impossible. What feels like conceiving it is always just a representation — a shell that looks like transcendence but remains safely inside fiction. You can never actually think the thought; you can only depict thinking it.
- Connected to metalepsis (Genette), strange loops (Hofstadter), conceivability/possibility distinction (Chalmers)
- The key distinction: the ontological argument has a "joint" (the bridge from concept to existence) that Kant can attack. SCP-3812 has no joint — conception = instantiation. But the price of collapsing the gap is that genuine conception becomes unreachable.
- **The exploit via unprobability:** The rule that prevents fiction from reaching across the ontological boundary into reality is precisely the kind of rule the First Theorem of Unprobability says can be violated. If unprobability operates at singularities (where rules break down), then the fiction/reality boundary might be among the rules that fail.
- **Connection to simulation:** If we're inside a computation, the fiction/reality boundary isn't ontological but computational. Computational boundaries can be breached.

**Why it qualifies:** The original entity concept is from djkaktus (SCP wiki). Sammy Martin's contribution is the philosophical diagnosis of why it almost works, where exactly it fails, and the connection to unprobability that turns the failure condition into a potential success condition.

**Source chats:** Understanding SCP-3812 (March 2026)

## Proposal 7: The Meta-Proposal (Historical Track Record)

**TRL: -1 (self-referentially)**
**Framework exploited:** Historical observation that speculative extrapolation from contested frameworks has a non-zero hit rate
**Core move:** The practice of generating TRL -1 to -2 ideas is itself valuable because the historical hit rate, while very low, is non-zero, and the payoff when one hits is disproportionately large.

**Key details:**
- Historical precedents: Democritus → nuclear weapons (~2,400 years), Hobbes → AGI (~375 years), Wells → Hiroshima (32 years)
- The relationship between ontological engineering and eventual real engineering is the same as the relationship between philosophy maturing into science
- Most proposals will be Empedocles (coherent, compelling, wrong). One might be Democritus.
- The practice is self-referentially at TRL -1: the very idea of systematising this is itself a contested-framework extrapolation. "It has worked before, and at TRL -1, that's enough."

**Corollary (AI acceleration):** If an AI system could generate millions of TRL -1/-2 proposals and filter for structural validity (not just style), the absolute number of hits could be non-trivial even with a tiny per-proposal hit rate. This is a more grounded and more unsettling version of the "AI accelerates physics" story — it's not about solving known problems faster, but about generating ideas at a level of abstraction where humans can barely operate, at a scale no human could match.

## Proposal 8: The Incompleteness Exploit (Credit: partially to SCP-3812 author djkaktus, refined by Sammy Martin)

**TRL: -2**
**Framework exploited:** SCP-3812 narrative concept + philosophy of fiction + ontological argument structure
**Core move:** An entity whose defining property is superseding everything that supersedes it, including its own fictional status, represents a concept where — if genuinely conceivable — conception and instantiation collapse into one event. The philosophical significance is in diagnosing exactly why this almost works and identifying where it connects to other frameworks (unprobability, simulation boundaries).

**Note:** This overlaps substantially with Proposal 6 and may be better understood as a sub-component of it rather than a separate proposal. The original concept is credited to djkaktus; the philosophical analysis and connections to unprobability are Sammy Martin's contribution.
