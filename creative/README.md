# Creative Writing

Fictional/creative pieces produced during the development of the FID concept.

## Files

### drive_sequence.md
**Heart of Gold — Master Systems Display: Infinite Improbability Drive Activation Sequence Log**

A detailed fictional systems log showing the IID activation sequence aboard the Heart of Gold, written in the style of the Sirius Cybernetics Corporation. Covers all five phases: pre-flight systems check, hyperspatial decoherence barrier deployment, finite improbability generation (observer creation and pruning), recursive amplification (tower generation reaching 2^276709:1 improbability), transit (with transient macro-scale improbability events including the canonical whale and petunias), and state vector lock-in. Includes a subsystem schematic.

This piece serves double duty as both pastiche and technical exposition — it explains the FID mechanism (observer generation, branch pruning, recursive amplification, decoherence isolation) through the framing of Adams' universe.

### improbability_scene.md
**"In Which Trillian Explains Something, and Arthur Wishes She Hadn't"**

A scene written in Adams' style where Trillian explains the Improbability Drive to Arthur Dent. Covers: the many-worlds interpretation ("the universe doesn't split — it defocuses"), the finite improbability generator (Sub-Meson Brain + quantum randomness + observer pruning), the transition to infinite improbability via recursive tower generation, the decoherence barrier, and the powers-of-two improbability figures as binary quantum branching. Functions as an accessible explanation of the FID theory through character dialogue.
