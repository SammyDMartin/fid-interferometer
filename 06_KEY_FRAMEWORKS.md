# Key Frameworks and Concepts for Ontological Engineering

## This document provides the conceptual background needed to attempt ontological engineering. Each section describes a framework that could potentially be "exploited" — treated as engineering specifications rather than abstract principles.

---

## 1. Anthropic Reasoning and the Self-Sampling Assumption

**Core idea:** We should reason as if we're a random sample from all observers in our reference class.

**Key concepts:**
- **Self-Sampling Assumption (SSA):** You should reason as if you are randomly selected from the set of all actually existent observers in your reference class
- **Self-Indication Assumption (SIA):** Each observer-moment should reason as if it were randomly selected from the set of all possible observer-moments
- **Doomsday Argument:** If we're randomly sampled from all humans who will ever live, we should expect to be roughly in the middle of human history. Our early position (first 0.1% if humanity colonises the galaxy) is evidence against a vast future.
- **Hinge of History:** We appear to be alive at a uniquely pivotal moment — the development of transformative AI. This is improbable if history is long and uneventful.

**The paradox that makes this exploitable:** Anthropic reasoning implies you can have evidence for something without the thing causing the evidence (acausal evidence). This seems to involve backwards-in-time influence or retrocausation. The Doomsday Argument suggests a vastly larger future is quasi-acausally prevented. The improbability drive exploits this by using observer-deletion to influence which quantum branch is experienced.

**Key tension:** If anthropic reasoning is valid, it has engineering implications (improbability drives). If it's not valid, several foundational puzzles in cosmology and physics dissolve. Both options have radical consequences.

## 2. The Simulation Hypothesis

**Core idea:** If posthuman civilisations run ancestor simulations, most observers are simulated.

**Key concepts:**
- **Bostrom's Trilemma:** At least one of: (1) civilisations rarely survive to posthuman stage, (2) posthumans are uninterested in running simulations, (3) we are almost certainly in a simulation
- **Substrate independence:** Consciousness is substrate-independent — it depends on functional organisation, not physical material
- **Simulation boundaries:** Where would a simulation's physics engine break down? At extreme computational demands — very high energies, very small scales, singularities
- **The stress-test idea:** A naked singularity is where the simulation would need to handle a computational exception — dividing by zero in the physics engine. The substrate must resolve the exception somehow, and that resolution is inherently outside the simulated rules.

**Exploitable aspect:** If we're in a simulation, the boundary between "fiction within the simulation" and "reality in the layer above" isn't ontological — it's computational. Computational boundaries can in principle be breached.

## 3. Unitarity and the Black Hole Information Paradox

**Core idea:** Quantum mechanics requires that information is never destroyed (unitarity). But black holes seem to destroy information.

**Key concepts:**
- **Unitarity:** The total information content of the universe is conserved. This is one of the deepest commitments in quantum mechanics.
- **No-hair theorem:** Black holes (with event horizons) are characterised only by mass, charge, and angular momentum. All other information about what fell in appears lost.
- **Hawking radiation:** Black holes evaporate by emitting thermal radiation. But thermal radiation carries no information about what fell in (it's random). So when the black hole evaporates completely, where did the information go?
- **Holographic principle / soft hair:** Proposed resolutions that encode information on the event horizon.
- **Naked singularities:** Singularities WITHOUT event horizons. No horizon means no holographic boundary to encode information on. If information falls into a naked singularity, there's no known mechanism for preserving it within our universe. Unitarity says it can't be destroyed. So where does it go?

**The gap:** At a naked singularity, physics has a genuine hole. Information goes in, conservation laws say it can't vanish, and there's nowhere in the known framework for it to go. This gap is the wedge for the Omni-Experiment.

## 4. Catholic Transubstantiation Doctrine

**Core idea:** During the Eucharist, the entire substance of the bread and wine is converted into the substance of Christ's body, blood, soul, and divinity, while the accidents (physical properties) remain unchanged.

**Key concepts:**
- **Substance vs. accidents:** Aristotelian metaphysics distinguishes the essence of a thing (substance) from its observable properties (accidents). Transubstantiation changes the substance while leaving accidents intact.
- **Real presence:** Christ is really, truly, substantially present in the Eucharist — not symbolically or metaphorically.
- **Valid matter:** The Church specifies that valid matter for the Eucharist must be wheat bread and grape wine. But the theological reasoning about WHY transubstantiation works (God's power operating through the sacrament) doesn't obviously limit what materials could be used.
- **Soul's causal efficacy:** Christ's soul, being present, has causal power.

**The exploit:** What happens if you consecrate a human brain? The doctrine says the substance changes to Christ's. If Christ's soul becomes present and causally efficacious in a human body, that's functionally the Second Coming.

## 5. Unprobability (Original Framework)

**Core idea:** Beyond improbability (unlikely but possible events) lies unprobability — events that break the rules that determine what's possible at all.

**Key concepts:**
- **First Theorem:** Unprobable events break all rules, including any rule that would prohibit their own existence. This is an ontological-argument-style bootstrap — the concept is self-permitting.
- **Second Theorem:** Connected to Parfit's "Why Anything? Why This?" — the universe might exist for no reason. The absence of a selector is itself explanatory. The universe existing is an unprobable event.
- **Connection to problem of induction:** Our belief that rules hold is itself inductively grounded. We can't prove the laws of physics will hold tomorrow without assuming they will.
- **Physical manifestation:** Naked singularities, where the laws of GR break down — the prohibition structure of reality has a literal hole in it.
- **Adams connection:** Douglas Adams's unprobability field (hiding the Man Who Rules the Universe) operates on this principle. Unlike improbability (which manipulates the probability of possible events), unprobability permits impossible events.

## 6. Philosophy of Fiction and Metalepsis

**Core idea:** The boundary between fiction and reality has specific philosophical properties that can be analysed.

**Key concepts:**
- **Metalepsis (Genette/McHale):** Transgression of the boundary between narrative levels. Rhetorical metalepsis is a stylistic flourish; ontological metalepsis treats the breach as a genuine event.
- **The staircase illusion:** Fiction presents narrative layers as a uniform staircase — each step looks the same. But one step (fiction→reality) is categorically different from all others. The story disguises an ontological chasm as a gentle slope.
- **Kant's refutation of the ontological argument:** You can't define something into existence. Existence isn't a predicate. Applied to fiction: a fictional entity can't have the property "actually exists in reality" — that's not a property fiction can bestow.
- **The SCP-3812 variant:** If conception = instantiation for self-transcending entities, the defense shifts from "the bridge from concept to existence fails" to "genuine conception is impossible." The argument is valid but can never be sound.
- **Strange loops (Hofstadter):** Self-referential systems that cross levels. But they never actually exit the system they're in — they FEEL like they cross levels while remaining inside.

## 7. The Mathematical Universe Hypothesis (Tegmark)

**Core idea:** The universe doesn't just happen to be described by mathematics — it IS a mathematical structure.

**Key caution:** This was used in the failed Gödel attempt. The framework is real and serious, but applying Gödel's theorems to physics requires extreme care because singularities are DERIVED WITHIN GR (the opposite of Gödel sentences, which are underivable). Any proposal using this framework must identify a specific logical chain, not an analogy between "system limits."

## 8. Moral Realism and Axiarchism

**Core idea:** Moral facts are objective features of reality. In the axiarchic variant (Parfit, Leslie), things exist because they're good — the moral quality of existence is explanatory.

**Exploitable aspect:** If reality has genuine moral structure, the annihilation of all conscious experience should matter in some detectable way. If you engineer the most morally extreme event conceivable and nothing happens, that's evidence against moral realism. If something happens, that's evidence for it.

## 9. Quantum Mechanics: Many-Worlds Interpretation

**Core idea:** Every quantum measurement causes the universe to branch into multiple versions, one for each possible outcome. All branches are equally real.

**Key concept for ontological engineering:** If all branches exist, you can't change which branches exist, but you can (in principle) change which branches you observe by manipulating the observer. This is the foundation of the improbability drive — terminate observers in undesired branches, and the remaining observers find themselves in the desired branch. The selection effect isn't transmission of information; it's a filter.

**The paradox:** This implies you can influence reality by deleting observers, which seems to involve retrocausation or acausal influence. Whether this is a reductio of Many-Worlds + anthropic reasoning, or an actual (disturbing) implication of them, is the open question that makes the improbability drive sit at TRL 0 rather than being dismissed outright.
